Module 2 · Challenge 9

Meeting Recovery

You don't need better notes. You need a faster way to turn the notes you already take into things that actually get done.

The Story

The meeting that evaporates by 3pm

You know this one: you leave a meeting with a page of scribbled notes, fully intending to "write it up properly later." Later comes, you open the notes, and half of it doesn't make sense anymore. Who was supposed to do the thing? Was that decided or just discussed?

The notes weren't the problem. The gap between taking them and doing something with them was the problem. That gap is exactly what today closes.

The move isn't "take better notes." It's: the moment the meeting ends, dump your raw notes in messy as they are, and let AI pull out what actually matters before you forget the context that made sense of them.

The Lesson

Separate the summary from the action items

A meeting produces two different things: what was discussed, and what someone now has to actually do. Mixing them together is how action items get lost inside paragraphs of context.

The move: paste your raw notes in as-is, right after the meeting, and ask for two separate things — a short summary, and a clearly separated list of action items with who owns each one, if that was said.

Try It Now

Vague vs. specific

Vague "Summarize this meeting." [pastes raw notes]

You'll get a paragraph. The action items are still buried inside it.

Specific "Here are my raw notes from today's meeting, messy: [paste notes]. Give me two things separately: a 3-sentence summary of what was discussed, and a bulleted list of action items — who owns each one if it was mentioned, and flag anything that sounded like a decision but wasn't clearly assigned."

Now nothing slips through — and you can see exactly what still needs an owner.

Your Challenge Today

Run your next meeting through this

Take notes from your next meeting — or a recent one still sitting in a notebook or notes app — and run them through the summary + action items pattern above.

⏱ Under an hour
Reflection

Before you move on

  • What surprised you about what it flagged as unassigned?
  • What worked — did separating the summary from the action items make things clearer?
  • What would you do differently — what would you note down differently in the meeting itself, knowing this is what happens after?
