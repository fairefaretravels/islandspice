# Learn How to Learn AI — Launch Playbook
*TikTok + YouTube Live launch, Day 1 tomorrow*

**Core message to repeat every session:** "I'm not teaching you ChatGPT. I'm teaching you how to learn any AI that comes out next year."

---

## PART 1 — DO THIS TONIGHT (before you sleep)

These two things are time-bombs. If you skip them, tomorrow's stream doesn't happen.

1. **Verify your YouTube channel phone number RIGHT NOW.**
   YouTube Studio → Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility → Intermediate features → Verify phone number. New/first-time live channels need up to a **24-hour** activation window after verifying before you can go live. Do this the moment you finish reading this — every hour you wait is an hour off tomorrow's window.

2. **Check your TikTok LIVE eligibility right now.**
   Open TikTok → tap **+** → swipe to **LIVE**. If the LIVE option is greyed out or missing, your account hasn't hit the threshold TikTok requires to unlock LIVE (in most regions this is followers-based, commonly cited around 1,000). This also gates whether you can pull a stream key for OBS or Prism.
   - **If LIVE is unlocked:** you're clear to broadcast natively from the app or via Prism/OBS with an RTMP key.
   - **If LIVE is locked:** don't burn tonight trying to unlock it. Plan B below covers this.

3. Charge both phones fully, confirm the backup phone actually has cellular data (not just Wi-Fi), and do one 5-minute test broadcast (private/unlisted) on each platform tonight so you're not debugging live tomorrow.

## PART 2 — Tomorrow's Streaming Path

Your gear: OBS (laptop), TikTok app (phone + laptop browser), Prism Live Studio (backup phone, cellular).

**Plan A — TikTok LIVE is unlocked:**
- **YouTube:** OBS on laptop → Custom RTMP → YouTube stream key (from Live Control Room). This is your primary, highest-quality feed — screen share + webcam + lesson slides.
- **TikTok:** Run this as a *second, independent broadcast* on the backup phone via Prism, pointed at you presenting (or angled to catch the laptop screen). Don't try to pipe TikTok through the same OBS instance unless you've tested a multi-RTMP setup (like a "Multiple RTMP Output" plugin) — one bad plugin config right before a launch is a real risk. Two separate devices broadcasting the same content is simpler and safer for a first live.
- Cellular backup phone = your insurance if home Wi-Fi hiccups; it also means TikTok's feed doesn't die if your laptop's connection does.

**Plan B — TikTok LIVE is still locked:**
- Go live on YouTube only tomorrow via OBS (this is guaranteed to work once your 24-hour window clears).
- On TikTok, post a native in-app **"I'm live on YouTube right now"** clip + link in bio, and clip highlights from the YouTube live into TikTok posts afterward. This builds toward TikTok's LIVE threshold instead of fighting it blind.
- Re-check TikTok LIVE eligibility daily — once unlocked, drop straight into Plan A for Day 2.

## PART 3 — The Curriculum: 5-Day Challenge

Renamed to Challenges per your instinct — it reframes this as something you *do*, not something you sit through.

### Challenge 1: Teach AI About You
*(Meet Your AI)*
- What AI is and isn't
- How to ask better questions
- **Project:** Have AI create your personal assistant prompt.

### Challenge 2: Save Yourself One Hour
*(AI for Everyday Life)*
- Planning, budgeting, meals, travel
- **Project:** Build an AI life planner.

### Challenge 3: Build Something You Need
*(AI for Work)*
- Emails, spreadsheets, documents
- **Project:** Create a week's worth of work in one afternoon.

### Challenge 4: Make Your First Dollar with AI
*(AI for Business)*
- Marketing, websites, social media
- **Project:** Launch a basic business presence.

### Challenge 5: Teach Someone Else
*(AI for Creativity — full circle close)*
- Images, music, video
- **Project:** Produce one complete piece of content — then teach one other person what you just learned. That's the whole philosophy in action: you didn't memorize a tool, you learned how to learn, and now you can pass it on.

**Format notes:**
- Each lesson resets in a single 30–90 min sitting — no dependency on "previous homework," so anyone can drop into any Challenge live.
- End every session with something tangible, not a quiz.

### Capstone Portfolio (instead of a test)
Students graduate with real assets, pick any that apply to them:
- Custom AI assistant · Business website · Content calendar · Résumé · Marketing campaign · Automation · Chatbot · Business plan

## PART 4 — Coaching Tiers + On-Air Promo Script

**Tiers to mention every session (say them in this order — low commitment to high):**
1. **Self-paced** — watch the lesson, complete the project, free/low-cost.
2. **Group coaching** — weekly live Q&A with you.
3. **1-on-1 coaching** — personalized help applying AI to *their* specific goal.

**Promo script — drop this around the 60-minute mark, after the Q&A, while energy is highest:**

> "If today's Challenge got your brain moving, here's how to go further. You can keep doing this self-paced — lesson, project, done. If you want accountability, group coaching gets you a live weekly Q&A with me. But if you've got a specific thing you're trying to build — your business, your side hustle, your résumé, whatever it is — 1-on-1 coaching is where we sit down and build *your* thing, not a generic example. I'm opening [X] spots for founding-rate 1-on-1 coaching to people watching live right now — link's in the bio/description, comment 'COACH' and I'll follow up personally."

**Scarcity/urgency lever (use honestly, not manufactured):** cap the founding-rate 1:1 spots to a real number (e.g., first 5 or 10) tied to your actual bandwidth this week, and say so plainly — "founding rate" framing works because it's early, not because it's fake-scarce.

**Closing-segment reminder line:** repeat the philosophy line one more time before you sign off — it's the thing people will remember and repeat when they tell others about the stream.
