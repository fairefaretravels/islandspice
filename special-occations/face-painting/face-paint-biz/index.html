Build a 2–3 Person Team

Your team can share the large equipment and main storage. Each painter should have their own personal workstation and supplies.

For EACH additional painter, purchase:
  • 1 three-compartment clear caddy
  • 1 apron
  • 1 personal supply of paper towels
  • 1 pack of antibacterial wipes
  • 1 personal set of clean paint-water cups
  • 1 personal brush set
  • Personal brush-cleaning and drying supplies

05. Three-Compartment Cleaning Station

Keep brush washing separate from paint activation.

Face painting workstation inspiration
Organized supply setup inspiration
01 — WASH Use soapy water to remove paint from brushes.
02 — RINSE Rinse brushes in clean water. Change dirty water regularly.
03 — FINAL RINSE Use a separate clean-water rinse. Then disinfect separately and allow brushes to dry completely.
Important sanitation practices
  • Keep paint activation water in separate clean cups.
  • Never mix alcohol into the caddy water.
  • Use an appropriate brush disinfectant according to its label.
  • Allow brushes to dry completely before reuse.
  • Use a fresh disposable sponge for each customer.
  • Never share contaminated brushes, sponges or water between customers.
